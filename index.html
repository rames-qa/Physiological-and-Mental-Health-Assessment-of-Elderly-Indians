Abstract

Psychological and physical health is very closely correlated with aged individuals. There are three primary facets of psychological contentment analyzed within this validation roadmap: evaluation contentment (life satisfaction), hedonic contentment (feeling of fulfillment), and eudaimonic contentment (feeling of core purpose in life). This assessment platform addresses systemic changes across elder profiles to evaluate how continuous physical measurements match corresponding psychological scales.
Keywords: ECG Features, Wearable Material Modules, Healthy Pi Acquisition, Mental Health Evaluation

Introduction & Engineering Context

Elderly self-reporting metrics of psychological well-being are turning into a core focus of broad health policy. As life anticipation increases and treatments for life-threatening chronic conditions grow more accessible, maintaining a high quality of life at advanced ages increases in significance. Prevalence of physical or functional issues often correlates closely with rising levels of depressed states.

This project establishes a clean, non-invasive diagnostic interface combining standard mental validation questionnaire protocols with underlying physical feature validation data. By monitoring cardiac performance and related biomarkers, the platform seeks data correlations signaling mental wellness shifts or depressive profiles.

Physiological Principles

Systolic Pressure (SBP): The contracting push as blood forces outward from the cardiac chambers.

Diastolic Pressure (DBP): The resting muscle relaxation phase receiving incoming blood flow back from lungs.

Cardiac Output Scale: The average human heart actively loops roughly 6,000 to 7,500 liters of blood per day.

Heart Mass Variation: Standard adult male organs scale near 280-340g; adult female profiles scale between 230-280g.

Cognitive Metrics

MMSE & MoCA: Validated tracking criteria designed to test short-term working recall, orientation, language processing, and general executive function levels up to a 30-mark maximum.

MNA Scoring: Used alongside physical mass ratios to evaluate standard baseline nutritional and structural wellness levels across aging populations.
