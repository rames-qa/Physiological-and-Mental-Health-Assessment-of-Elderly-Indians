Abstract

Psychological and physical health is very closely correlated with aged individuals. Three primary facets of psychological contentment are analyzed within this validation roadmap: evaluation contentment (life satisfaction), hedonic contentment (feeling of fulfillment), and eudaimonic contentment (feeling of purpose in life). This system assesses shifts across elder profiles to evaluate how continuous physical parameters align with underlying psychological models.
Keywords: ECG Data, Wearable Materials, Healthy Pi Acquisition, Mental Health Evaluation

Physiological Principles

Systolic (SBP): The contracting push as blood forces outward from the cardiac chambers.

Diastolic (DBP): The resting muscle relaxation phase receiving incoming blood flow back from lungs.

Cardiac Capacity: The human heart loops roughly 6,000 to 7,500 liters of blood per day.

Heart Mass Variation: Adult male organs scale near 280-340g; adult female profiles scale between 230-280g.

Cognitive Metrics

MMSE & MoCA: Validated tracking criteria designed to test short-term working recall, orientation, language processing, and general executive function levels up to a 30-mark maximum.

MNA Scoring: Used alongside physical mass ratios to evaluate standard baseline nutritional and structural wellness levels across aging populations.
